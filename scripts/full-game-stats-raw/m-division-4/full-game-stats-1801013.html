Commentaire

Rebound Pr'izerdaul B (PREB) beat Black Frogs Schieren C (FROC) 62-53 in Bettborn after a steady, defense-first game under FIBA rules. The opening quarter was tight (15-15): FROC jumped 1-6, but PREB answered with a 6-0 run sparked by Welton Djon Silva do Rosario (nine points in the period).

PREB struck first after the break between quarters with a 7-0 start to Q2 (22-15). FROC replied through Noah Michels with four points in a row to close the gap, and Bob Hirt trimmed it further from the line to 29-27 at halftime. The turning point arrived in Q3: from 30-29, PREB pieced together a 13-2 surge featuring a Devin Alves Neves triple and inside finishes from Silva do Rosario and Aaron Legg, pushing the largest lead to +10 (39-29) and 43-37 before the last act.

FROC opened Q4 with a 4-0 spurt to 43-39, but PREB immediately countered 5-0 to 50-39. Even after a technical on Aaron Legg at 50-39, coach Weyer's team steadied with a 6-0 run (56-42). Michels hit a late three and Hirt knocked down consecutive free throws, yet PREB managed the closing minutes without allowing a true comeback.

Standouts PREB: Welton Djon Silva do Rosario (30, 1.94m, PRT) 16 points; Devin Alves Neves (24, PRT) 15, including three straight free throws late; Zachary Legg (24) 13; captain PEDRO BATISTA Jean-Luc 9. For FROC: Bob Hirt 14, Michels (29, 1.80m) 13 with a pair of second-quarter baskets and a late three, and captain Jerry Valente 8.

Aaron Legg navigated foul trouble (four fouls plus a Q4 technical) but stayed on court. With this result, PREB climbs to 3rd place (1-1), while FROC sits 4th (1-1).
