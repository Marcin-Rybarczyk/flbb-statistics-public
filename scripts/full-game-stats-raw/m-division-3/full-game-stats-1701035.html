Commentaire

BBC East Side Pirates B overturned a 17-point deficit to beat AS Soleuvre C, 60-52, in a game defined by swings and late-game composure. Soleuvre blitzed the start with a 15-0 opening run and took the first quarter 17-4. They maintained control to halftime (35-20) and even stretched the gap to 37-20 early in the third, the game's highest lead.

From there BEPB flipped the script. Adomas Gavenas (27, DEU) ignited the comeback with 10 points in the third quarter as the hosts won the period 18-7 to trail only 38-42 entering the fourth. The final frame belonged to the Pirates' guards: Christophe Conter (30) hit a pair of key threes (one in Q2, one early Q4) and then calmly drained five free throws in a row amid a tense sequence that included a technical on Pit Heinz and, later, an unsportsmanlike on Soleuvre's coach Tim Geiben plus a bench technical. BEPB closed a 15-2 run to make it 60-49 before Tom Lahure's late three set the final score.

Top performers
- BEPB: Gavenas led all scorers with 23. Local support was strong from Claude Schroeder (41) with 13 and Conter's 14, including those clutch fourth-quarter points. Player-coach Tony Martins Ferreira (Season PPG: 3.14) added a late free throw and steadied the group.
- SOLC: Veteran Tom Lahure (45) tallied 19 and carried the offense early; Ousmane Maiga (25) added 9. Starters Charel Plumat (28) and Jeremy Le Marchand (33) fouled out in the fourth, hurting the visitors' finish.

Game flow notes
- Soleuvre opened on a 15-0 tear.
- BEPB's second half was 40-17, with the decisive 22-10 fourth quarter.
- Conter scored five consecutive points from the line in the clutch.

In the standings, BEPB climb to 1st (3-1, +3 places) and are on a three-game winning streak. Soleuvre sit 6th (1-3) after a second straight loss.
