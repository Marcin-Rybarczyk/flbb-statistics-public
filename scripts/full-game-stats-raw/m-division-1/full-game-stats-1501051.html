Commentaire

Racing Luxembourg C overturned a seven-point halftime deficit to win 89-78 at Deichwisen/Central, powered by a dominant second half and a decisive third-quarter surge. Etzella Ettelbruck B raced out 33-26 in the first quarter, sparked by captain Miguel Angelo Domingos Sanches (Season PPG: 24.0), who drilled three consecutive triples in the late minutes to create a nine-point cushion. The hosts kept control to the break, 50-43.

Tom Muller's side flipped the script after halftime. From 56-47 for ETZB, Racing stitched together a 9-0 run to tie, absorbed a brief ETZB basket, then struck with a second 10-0 burst to close the third up 66-60. The visitors kept their foot down in the fourth, stretching the lead to a game-high +15 (74-89) before closing it out.

Sofiane Amadou Bah (Season PPG: 10.8) led Racing with 23 points, while Marcin Rybarczyk (Season PPG: 6.2) and Alexis Gumy (34, CHE) added 18 apiece. Miguel Llevenes Martinez (33, ESP) chipped in 11 and hit early perimeter shots that steadied Racing's offense.
