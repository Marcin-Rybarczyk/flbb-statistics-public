Commentaire

Racing Luxembourg B controlled Etzella Ettelbruck B 94-79 in the M-Division 1 championship round at tramsschap/central, after a game that swung decisively in the third quarter. Etzella started sharper, edging the first period 18-14, with Miguel Angelo Domingos Sanches (20, 1.80m, PRT) closing the quarter on three straight free throws. In the second, Etzella pushed the margin to 37-29 on the shooting of Jos Miguel Domingos Sanches (19, 1.76m, PRT), but Racing rallied to 43-41 at halftime, sparked by Xavier Engel (37) and two late free throws by Mathieu Gillardin (34, 2.05m).

The game turned after the break. Etzella opened the third with a 9-0 burst and soon led 53-45, forcing coach Pit Schneider to stop the bleeding early.
