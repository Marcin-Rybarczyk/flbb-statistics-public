📋 Methodology

How is RPI calculated?

  • Fairness Score (30%): Measures balance in foul calls between home and away teams. Lower foul differential = higher score.
  • Consistency Score (30%): Measures variance in fouls called per game. Lower variance = higher score.
  • Game Control Score (25%): Evaluates technical/unsportsmanlike foul rates. Optimal technical foul rate around 7.5%.
  • Experience Score (15%): Based on number of games officiated with logarithmic scaling for diminishing returns.

Final RPI = Weighted average of all four scores, normalized to 0-100 scale.
