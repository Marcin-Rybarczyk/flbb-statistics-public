Player Game Impact Analysis

Players ranked by overall impact on games - considering scoring, win rate, efficiency, and team performance

📊 Understanding the Metrics

  • Impact Score: A comprehensive weighted metric combining scoring (30%), win rate (25%), shooting efficiency (20%), team performance differential (15%), starting importance (10%), minus a foul penalty. Higher scores indicate greater overall game impact.
  • Win Rate: Percentage of games won when this player participates, indicating their correlation with team success.
  • Team Performance: Average point differential (team score minus opponent score) when the player plays. Positive values indicate the team outscores opponents.

Player Foul Impact Analysis

Analysis of how fouls affect game outcomes and player effectiveness

⚠️ Understanding Foul Impact

  • Foul Impact (Team Rate Change): Percentage change in team's total foul rate when this player is on the court compared to when they're not. Positive values indicate the team commits more fouls with this player, negative values indicate fewer fouls.
  • Fouls/Game: Average personal fouls committed by the player per game. Lower values indicate better discipline.
  • Win Rate w/ Fouls: Team's win percentage in games where this player commits fouls, showing whether their aggressive play style correlates with team success.

👥 Understanding Synergy Score

Referee Game Impact Analysis

Analysis of referee influence on game dynamics, scoring, and foul patterns

🟣 Understanding Referee Impact Metrics

  • Score Impact (League Deviation): How much the average total game score deviates from the league average when this referee officiates. Positive values indicate higher-scoring games, negative values indicate lower-scoring games.
  • Foul Impact (League Deviation): How much the average fouls per game deviates from the league average with this referee. Positive values mean more fouls are called (stricter officiating), negative values mean fewer fouls (more lenient officiating).
  • Overall Impact (Competitiveness): Measures how much the rate of close games (decided by 5 points or less) deviates from the league average. Positive values indicate this referee's games are more competitive (closer scores), while negative values indicate more lopsided games.
  • Interpretation: These metrics help identify refereeing styles - some refs allow more physical play (negative foul impact), while others maintain tighter control (positive foul impact). Score and competitiveness impacts show how referee decisions influence game flow and outcomes.
