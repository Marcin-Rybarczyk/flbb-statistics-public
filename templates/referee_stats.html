About Referee Statistics

Understanding the Metrics

  • Games Refereed: Total number of games officiated by each referee
  • Avg Fouls/Game: Average number of fouls called per game (shared among all referees in a game)
  • Close Games: Games decided by 5 points or less
  • High Scoring: Games with total score over 100 points
  • Referee Impact: Analysis of game patterns and characteristics when specific referees officiate
