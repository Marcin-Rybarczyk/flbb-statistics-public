🏠 Home & Standings

The home page and standings section provide an overview of team rankings across different divisions.

Features:

  • Division Selection: Choose from available divisions to view specific standings
  • Team Rankings: View teams ranked by points, with wins, losses, and point differentials
  • Quick Overview: See win/loss records and total points for each team
  • Team Logos: Visual representation of teams with their official logos

How to Use:

  1. Navigate to Home or Standings from the navigation menu
  2. Select a division from the dropdown menu
  3. Click "Show Standings" to view the current rankings
  4. Teams are automatically sorted by points (wins count for 2 points)

📅 Fixtures

The fixtures page shows the complete schedule of games, including past results and upcoming matches.

Features:

  • Game Schedule: Comprehensive list of all games with dates and times
  • Match Results: View final scores for completed games
  • Division Filtering: Filter fixtures by specific divisions
  • Game Matrix: Visual representation of head-to-head matchups
  • Venue Information: See where each game is/was played

How to Use:

  1. Click on Fixtures in the navigation menu
  2. Use the division filter to narrow down to specific competitions
  3. Browse through the list of games chronologically
  4. Click on individual games for detailed information

🎮 Game Statistics

Comprehensive game-level statistics and highlights across the league.

Features:

  • Highest Scoring Games: See games with the most combined points
  • Biggest Wins: Identify games with the largest point differentials
  • Biggest Leads: Track the largest leads held during games
  • Tie Scores: Games with the most tied scores throughout
  • Lead Changes: Most competitive games by number of lead changes
  • Game Duration: Longest and shortest games by playing time
  • Division Filtering: View statistics for specific divisions

How to Use:

  1. Access Game Stats from the navigation (requires login)
  2. Select a division or view all divisions
  3. Browse through different statistical categories
  4. Each statistic shows top games with relevant details

👤 Player Statistics

Detailed individual player performance metrics and rankings.

Features:

  • Top Scorers: Leading players by total points and per-game averages
  • Single Game Highs: Best individual game performances
  • Three-Point Leaders: Best three-point shooters
  • Shooting Efficiency: Field goal percentages and efficiency ratings
  • Foul Statistics: Players with most fouls and foul patterns
  • Consistency Metrics: Players who consistently score in double digits
  • Player Detail View: Deep dive into individual player statistics
  • Hover Information: Quick stats preview on player name hover

How to Use:

  1. Navigate to Player Stats (requires login)
  2. Browse different statistical categories
  3. Click on Player Detail for comprehensive individual stats
  4. Search for specific players using the search function
  5. Hover over player names for quick stat previews

🏀 Team Statistics

Comprehensive team performance analysis and comparative statistics.

Features:

  • Team Performance: Win/loss records, points for/against
  • Offensive Stats: Scoring averages, highest scores, shooting percentages
  • Defensive Stats: Points allowed, defensive efficiency
  • Home/Away Splits: Performance comparison at home vs. away
  • Winning Streaks: Current and longest winning/losing streaks
  • Team Detail View: In-depth team analysis with game-by-game breakdown
  • Player Rosters: View team rosters and player contributions
  • Division Filtering: Compare teams within specific divisions

How to Use:

  1. Go to Team Stats (requires login)
  2. Select a division to filter teams
  3. Browse team performance metrics
  4. Click Team Detail for comprehensive team analysis
  5. Select a team from the dropdown for detailed statistics

👨‍⚖️ Referee Statistics

Analysis of referee performance and game management statistics.

Features:

  • Games Officiated: Total number of games per referee
  • Foul Statistics: Average fouls called per game
  • Most/Least Fouls: Referees who call most and least fouls
  • Referee Performance Index: Comprehensive performance scoring
  • Game Impact Analysis: How referee decisions affect game flow
  • Referee Detail View: Complete statistics for individual referees
  • Hover Information: Quick referee stats on name hover

How to Use:

  1. Navigate to Referee Stats (requires login)
  2. View overall referee statistics and rankings
  3. Click Referee Performance Index for detailed performance metrics
  4. Use Referee Detail to analyze specific referees
  5. Select a referee from the dropdown for comprehensive statistics

🔍 Deep Analysis

Advanced analytics and deeper insights into basketball performance.

Features:

  • Starting Five vs Bench: Compare starter and bench player contributions
  • Double-Digit Scorers: Games with most players scoring 10+ points
  • Consistent Scorers: Players with high double-digit scoring frequency
  • Player Combinations: Best performing player pairings
  • Game Impact Analysis: How individual players affect game outcomes
  • Foul Impact Analysis: Relationship between fouls and performance
  • Shooting Efficiency: Advanced shooting metrics and percentages
  • Division Filtering: Focus analysis on specific divisions

How to Use:

  1. Access Deep Analysis from the navigation (requires login)
  2. Select a division for focused analysis or view all divisions
  3. Explore various advanced analytics sections
  4. Each section provides detailed tables with sortable columns
  5. Use insights to understand team dynamics and player performance patterns

⚙️ Preferences

Customize your experience with personal preferences and settings.

Features:

  • Preferred Division: Set default division for automatic filtering
  • Preferred Team: Set default team for team detail pages
  • Theme Selection: Choose from multiple color schemes
  • Session Persistence: Preferences saved for your browsing session

Available Themes:

  • Default Purple: Classic purple gradient theme
  • Ocean Blue: Calming blue color scheme
  • Sunset Orange: Warm orange and red tones
  • Forest Green: Natural green theme
  • Minimal Gray: Clean and simple grayscale
  • Cherry Red: Bold red color scheme

How to Use:

  1. Navigate to Preferences (requires login)
  2. Select your preferred division (optional)
  3. Select your preferred team (optional)
  4. Choose a theme color
  5. Click "Save Preferences" to apply your settings
  6. Your preferences will be used as defaults on applicable pages

🏀 Game Details

Search and view comprehensive information about specific games.

Features:

  • Game Search: Find games by ID, team names, or division
  • Detailed Statistics: Complete game breakdown with quarter-by-quarter scores
  • Player Performance: Individual player statistics for the game
  • Play-by-Play: Detailed game events and timeline
  • Referee Information: Officials who worked the game
  • Venue Details: Location and attendance information

How to Use:

  1. Click on Game Details (requires login)
  2. Use the search function to find specific games
  3. Enter game ID, team names, or filter by division
  4. Click on a game to view comprehensive details
